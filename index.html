Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about our deposit schemes

A Nidhi Company is a type of Indian financial entity that is registered under the Companies Act and operates under the Nidhi Rules. It is authorized to borrow money from its members (who are also shareholders) and lend to members only. Nidhi companies are regulated by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and RBI guidelines.

Yes, FLP Nidhi Limited is a registered Nidhi Company operating under the Companies Act. We follow all RBI and MCA guidelines for Nidhi Companies. Your deposits are secured with our company, and we have a track record of 15+ years of safe and timely returns.

You need: 1) Aadhaar Card (KYC) 2) PAN Card 3) Passport Size Photo 4) Address Proof (if different from Aadhaar) 5) Mobile Number linked to Aadhaar. The process is simple and can be completed within 15-30 minutes.

Your maturity amount will be credited to your account within 30 days from the maturity date. For recurring deposits, you can also opt for auto-renewal or credit to your savings account linked with the deposit.

Yes, premature withdrawal is allowed. The interest rate applicable will be 1-2% lower than the contracted rate for the period the deposit remained with us. Please contact our office for detailed withdrawal process and applicable terms.

You can make deposits through: 1) Cash at our office 2) UPI/Online Transfer to our account 3) Cheque deposit 4) Through our Mobile App 5) Standing instruction from your bank account. You'll receive instant confirmation via SMS.
